# 夜间直播人物与场景选型 / Nighttime casting concepts

本轮五款全部采用封闭室内、夜晚、人工照明。人物为五位不同的虚构成年女性；各自的直播机位、造型与背景不同。

All five concepts show different fictional adult women in enclosed rooms at night. The selected Sub2API gpt-image-2.5 high-quality route generated the images. Candidates 01–03 are newly generated camera recompositions of their V2 adult identity references. Candidate 03 specifically cosplays Clove (暮蝶) from VALORANT, using official Riot artwork as additional costume-only references. Candidates 04 and 05 retain their reviewed V2 image outputs. Platform references and access limitations are recorded in camera-research.json; framing angles are design targets, not measured camera calibration.

打开 `selection.html` 可离线放大查看、加入候选、导出选型记录。`night-selection-overview.jpg` 是对比总览。每款包含生成原始 PNG、720×1280 竖屏交付版、JPG 预览和实际编辑提示词；`catalog.json` 和 CSV 包含机位、场景、审核说明与 SHA-256。

These are static character-and-scene concepts for selection. Matching body videos, voice designs and lip-sync preparation have not been produced for these five candidates. Current 女A / 林知夏 remains the active live character. Resized files and the contact sheet are derivatives of the five actual model outputs.
